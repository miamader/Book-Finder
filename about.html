About BookFinder

A virtual library for readers and writers.

BookFinder is a platform where users can discover books, explore genres, follow authors, and publish their own stories chapter by chapter.

What you can do

Readers can browse books, search by genre or author, and find highly rated titles. Writers can create books, organize them into series, and continue building their stories over time.

Our goal

Our goal is to make reading and self-publishing feel simple, accessible, and community-driven. BookFinder was designed as a capstone project focused on creating a clean experience for both readers and authors.
